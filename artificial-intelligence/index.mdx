# Artificial Intelligence

This knowledge base covers the foundations, methods, technologies, and engineering practices used to build intelligent systems.

## Learning paths

- **AI foundations:** mathematics, programming, data, and classical AI.
- **Machine Learning Engineer:** classical ML, feature engineering, evaluation, deep learning, and MLOps.
- **Generative AI Engineer:** transformers, LLMs, embeddings, RAG, multimodal AI, and agents.
- **AI specialization:** NLP, computer vision, speech, recommender systems, reinforcement learning, or robotics.

Start with the foundations, then follow the learning path most relevant to your goals.
